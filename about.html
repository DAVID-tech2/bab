Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Service and Community

Babalanda Foundation is a voluntary, community development organisation associated with Hon. Babirye Milly Babalanda, Minister for the Presidency of the Republic of Uganda, with a strong connection to the Busoga sub-region of Eastern Uganda.

Our role is to support and complement community service, empowerment, and sustainable development initiatives. We work alongside local communities, leaders, and partners to identify needs and deliver meaningful, lasting impact.

From youth empowerment and women's livelihoods to education, health, and community development, the Foundation is dedicated to touching lives and creating positive change where it matters most.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

The principles that guide our work and our commitment to communities.

Our Vision

A Transformed and empowered society where every individual has the opportunity tolive a dignified, healthy and prosperous life.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable and underserved communities through sustainable initiatives in education, healthcare, livelihoods, youth development, and social welfare, creating lasting opportunities for individuals and communities to thrive.
