Who We Are

A Foundation Dedicated to Community Transformation

Babalanda Foundation is a voluntary, community development organisation committed to serving the people of Busoga and beyond. We work alongside communities to create lasting, positive change through empowerment, service, and sustainable development.

Our approach is rooted in understanding local needs and working hand-in-hand with community members, leaders, and partners to deliver meaningful impact across key areas of development.

A Commitment to Service

Driven by a Passion for People

At Babalanda Foundation, we believe that meaningful change begins with a commitment to serving people and strengthening communities. Our work is guided by a deep belief in the dignity, potential, and resilience of every individual. Through our programmes and initiatives, we seek to create opportunities, support vulnerable communities, empower young people and families, and contribute to sustainable development across the Busoga sub-region and beyond. We remain committed to working together with communities, partners, and stakeholders to address the challenges facing our people and build a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive. This Foundation is more than an organisation—it is a commitment to service, compassion, integrity, and lasting impact. Together, we can make a difference.

The Babalanda Foundation draws inspiration from the values of service, integrity, and community that guide its work across the Busoga sub-region and beyond.

Through the Foundation's programmes and initiatives, the commitment to uplifting communities remains at the heart of everything we do.
