What We Do

Six Areas of Impact

Our programmes address the most pressing needs of communities across Busoga and beyond.

Youth Empowerment

Skills development, mentorship and opportunities for young people. We support youth with vocational training, entrepreneurship guidance, and personal development programmes.

Learn More

Women Empowerment

Supporting women and strengthening livelihoods and participation. We work with women's groups to build capacity, foster economic independence, and promote active community participation.

Learn More

Education

Supporting education and learning opportunities for communities. We work to improve access to quality education through school support, learning materials, and educational initiatives.

Learn More

Community Development

Supporting initiatives that improve communities and livelihoods. We invest in community infrastructure, access to clean water, and initiatives that enhance quality of life.

Learn More

Health & Wellbeing

Community-focused health and wellbeing initiatives. We support health outreach programmes, awareness campaigns, and access to essential health services in underserved areas.

Learn More

Livelihoods

Supporting sustainable economic opportunities for communities. We promote sustainable agriculture, entrepreneurship, and income-generating activities that build resilient livelihoods.

Learn More
